Writing and ideas

Writing on hard tech, energy, defense and the markets that finance them, worked from the step that physically limits a system and the invoice that pays for it. Every piece shows its numbers so you can rerun them.

  1. Who waits for the bladesNovember 1, 2026

    SpaceX says it will end 2027 with 13 gigawatts of compute, and it is building a blade foundry to get there. Boom built a turbine factory and lost the customer it built it for. Run the numbers and the blade shortage does not stop the highest bidder. It decides whose chips sit dark. Part two of two.

  2. The power problem is a casting problemNovember 1, 2026

    Gas turbine makers are sold out to 2031 and the price of a turbine has nearly tripled since 2019. Work backward from the chips and the constraint turns out to be a foundry, and the arithmetic says time is worth far more than efficiency. Part one of two. Part two is about who gets the blades.
